Rules

  • Movement. Men move one square diagonally forward. They capture both forward and backward.
  • Mandatory capture. If a capture exists, it must be taken. When several captures are possible, the sequence that takes the most pieces is required.
  • Flying kings. Kings move and capture any distance along a diagonal.
  • Promotion. A man becomes a king only if the move ends on the far row. Passing through that row in a multi-capture does not crown it.
  • Winning. Capture every opposing piece, or leave the opponent with no legal move.
  • Draws. Threefold repetition of the same position, or king endgames of 1 vs 1, 2 vs 1, or 3 vs 1.
